Privacy policy

Last updated: January 2025

This privacy policy describes how Jade Palast (“we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses and shares personal data when you visit our website or place an order.

1. Personal data we collect

We collect personal data that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Name, email address, phone number and delivery address (required for orders)
  • Payment information (securely processed through our payment provider)
  • Communication preferences and marketing consent
  • Information you provide when contacting us (e.g. reservation, catering or event inquiries)

We automatically collect certain information when you visit our website:

  • IP address, browser type and device information
  • Pages visited, time spent on each page and referring URLs
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see our cookie policy)

2. How we use your data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Processing and fulfilling your orders
  • Communicating with you about orders and inquiries
  • Sending marketing communications (with your consent)
  • Improving our website and services
  • Fulfilling legal obligations
  • Managing our loyalty programme (Rewards Club)
  • Processing reservation requests
  • Handling catering and event requests

3. Legal basis for processing

We process your data on the following grounds:

  • Contract fulfilment: processing orders and providing services
  • Consent: marketing communication and cookies
  • Legitimate interests: improving our services and preventing fraud
  • Legal obligation: tax and accounting requirements

4. Data sharing

We may share your data with trusted third-party providers who help us operate our website, process payments and deliver orders. These partners are contractually obliged to keep your data secure.

Our online ordering platform is operated by FoodAmigos, which acts as a data processor on our behalf.

5. Your rights

In accordance with applicable data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Information about the personal data we have stored
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion of your data
  • Objection to or restriction of processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw your consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

6. Data storage

We only store your personal data for as long as is necessary to fulfil the purposes stated in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

7. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how to exercise your rights, please contact us at info@jadepalast.de.
