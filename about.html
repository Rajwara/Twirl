Our story

Mr. Ye came to Germany from China more than 30 years ago. Without speaking the language, far away from his homeland, his family and everything he knew and loved.

Job hunting was hard without language skills or formal training. But Mr. Ye didn't give up — he knew a family was counting on him. He finally found work in a kitchen, starting at the very bottom as a dishwasher, and there he discovered his passion for cooking.

After more than 30 years of experience and dedication, he started his passion project: hand-pulled noodles with the taste of his hometown, Qiantian, made just the way his parents used to make them for him. Today he shares the taste of his childhood with devotion at Jade Palast, the restaurant for authentic Chinese cuisine in the heart of Berlin-Charlottenburg.
